Jinkyong (Jin) Choi

Jin is a fifth-year PhD candidate in Management at Columbia Business School. She studies entrepreneurship and strategy, asking how the world around founders shapes what they choose to build. Her current work examines whether generative AI, by making it easier to turn ideas into products, leads entrepreneurs to explore a wider range of opportunities or to converge on similar ones. She is also interested in how founders’ social environments shape the opportunities they recognize. Across her research, Jin hopes to help entrepreneurs identify and act on growth opportunities.

Jin’s interest began with her professional experience at the Korea Communications Commission, where she was responsible for developing the Korean government’s AI ethics charter. Conversations with innovators in the AI industry made her curious about why they chose to pursue some ideas over others, which drew her to academia. Before joining Columbia, she worked with Luigi Zingales at the Stigler Center at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business.

Research interests

  • Artificial intelligence and entrepreneurial strategy
  • Innovation and new venture growth
  • Places, social capital, and entrepreneurship
